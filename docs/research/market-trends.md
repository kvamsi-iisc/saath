| Online matrimony | 1,200 to 1,400 crore | 2,300 to 2,500 crore | 10 to 12% CAGR |
| Online dating | | 2,000 to 2,500 crore | |
| Offline matrimony (brokers, agencies) | 4,500 to 5,000 crore | | 4 to 5% |
| **Diaspora dating plus matrimony** | | 1,000 to 1,200 crore | 11 to 13% CAGR |

Other Redseer findings: diaspora couples meeting online rises from 15% to 27% by 2030;
women are ~26% of dating-app users; core base is 25 to 34; **over 75% of Gen Z report
swiping fatigue**; a swipe app lost 7% of paying users YoY in Q3 2025 while "serious dating"
apps gained 17%; **only 5 to 15% of monthly actives convert to premium**; diaspora users
pay more and retain better.

**[I]** The incumbents' online matrimony pie is small and slow. The growth is in the
diaspora, in serious-intent products, and in the offline broker spend that a trusted
digital product could absorb. Offline is three to four times the size of online.

## 2. What prospective brides and grooms want now

### Jeevansathi "The Big Shift" report, Feb 2026 (2016 to 2025 data, 30,000 in-app surveys) [V]
- Median user age rose from 27 to 29. Half start searching at 29.
- Strict caste preference fell from **91% to 54%** overall, 49% in metros.
- Remarriage seekers rose from 11% to 16%. 15% of interest in divorced profiles comes from
  never-married users.
- **77% of profiles are self-created and self-managed**, up from 67% in 2016. Siblings are
  more involved than parents in Tier 3.
- 90% say the right partner matters more than the right age or finances.
- 87% of men are fine with a wife earning more; only 15% of women accept a husband earning less.
- 78% want to marry within 6 months, 48% within 3.
Caveat: single platform, self-selected sample.

### Other signals
- Tinder India Year in Swipe 2025: engagement up 13%, themes of clarity and honesty over
  mixed signals. [V]
- Bumble India (Sep 2024, 2,000 singles): 94% say anxiety about the future shapes how they
  date; 62% of women rank emotional stability first; 70% of women say they no longer
  compromise; a third practise "slow dating". [V]
- Shaadi.com "India's Most Eligible" (2023): men earning 30 lakh plus are 190% more desired;
  government jobs most desired; non-working women 31% less desired. No 2025 or 2026 survey found. [V]
- Telugu-states survey (Jul 2026): over 90% of profiles prioritise caste or sub-caste;
  under 10% choose "caste no bar"; IT clusters are the most caste-indifferent. [V]
- Urban self-arranged marriages: ~10% inter-caste, ~5% inter-religion, versus under 5% and
  1.5% for parent-arranged (2026 ScienceDirect study, non-random sample). [V]
- **[I] Caste no bar is growing as a stated filter in metros, far less as behaviour.**
  Keep caste and religion as optional user-chosen filters. Removing them loses the family;
  forcing them loses the metro professional.

### Astrology and compatibility
- 53.8% of Gen Z sought astrological guidance, mostly on relationships (Astroyogi, 2023,
  500k sample). Astrotalk runs ~70 crore rupees per month with free kundli matching as the
  acquisition hook. [S]
- Pre-marital counselling is spreading among urban couples including arranged matches.
  Genetic compatibility testing is an emerging niche (The Week, Jun 2026). [S]
- No Prepare/Enrich or Gottman India adoption data found.
- **[I]** Offer kundli matching as an optional module next to evidence-based compatibility.
  The market is converging on the hybrid.

### Segments the incumbents under-serve
- **Divorced, widowed, single parents, late 30s:** Jeevansathi 16% share is the only hard
  number. Incumbents bolt on separate brands (SecondShaadi, Divorcee Matrimony).
  New entrant SecondSutra targets this with virtual meets. [V/S]
- **Diaspora:** Dil Mil (~1,000 signups/day self-reported), Sirf Coffee (bespoke, ~10k
  members), Shaadi and BharatMatrimony NRI verticals priced in USD. Highest ARPU segment. [V/S]
- **LGBTQ:** Matrimony.com runs RainbowLuv (2022, selfie-verified, 11 to 13M addressable
  estimate). Same-sex marriage still unrecognised in India. [V]
- **Video and voice profiles:** industry says top apps are adding them as authenticity
  signals; no measured catfish-reduction data. [S]

## 3. Incumbent economics and pricing

### Matrimony.com (BharatMatrimony, listed) [V, Entrackr, ICICI Direct, transcripts]
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| FY26 revenue | ~460 crore rupees, flat |
| FY26 profit after tax | 34 crore, down 24% |
| Advertising and marketing | 44.7 crore in Q4 FY26, **38% of revenue** |
| Paid profile additions FY25 | ~9.95 lakh, down 7.3% |
| Average transaction value Q3 FY26 | ~5,144 rupees, up 13% YoY |
| Market cap | ~920 crore |
| Assisted Service rate card | $520 for 3 months, $935 for 6 months |

**[I]** Volume is shrinking and price is rising. More than a third of revenue goes to
buying traffic. An incumbent that spends 38% on marketing and still loses paid users is
not winning on product. Their Aadhaar approval (Aug 2026) shows they know trust is the
next battleground.

### Shaadi.com (People Group, private) [S]
About 4,500 rupees for 3 months, 7,500 for 6, 11,500 for 12. VIP and relationship-manager
tiers from 40,000 rupees. Announced queer matchmaking.

### Jeevansathi (Info Edge) [S]
"Till marriage" plans: Pro 9,830, Pro Max 13,898, Pro Supreme 19,457 rupees list, heavily
discounted (2,458 to 4,865 shown). Assisted 6-month 50,847 list, 15,255 discounted.
Also sells pay-per-contact.

### Benchmarks from elsewhere [V/S]
- Match Group revenue per payer ~$21 per month.
- Invite-only models: Raya ~$20 to 25 per month plus committee gate; Lox Club $96 per year;
  The League tiered limits. All flat fee plus scarcity, no success fee.
- Jodi365 (India) hybrid: 50% upfront, 50% success fee. US matchmakers $1,295 to 2,700 per
  month. Only ~5% of matchmakers use success fees.

## 4. Business-model conclusion [I]

Verified-only **entry gating** fits matrimony well: serious-intent apps are growing payers
by 17%, families value trust above all, and the incumbent just secured Aadhaar
authentication precisely to sell a "verified" indicator. Pure social-status gating
(Raya-style) conflicts with family-driven, community-wide search.

Recommended ladder:
1. **Free verified tier.** Identity and photo verification are free and mandatory. The
   verification is the product, not the paywall. Charging for the badge recreates the
   incumbents' two-class system of verified and unverified profiles.
2. **Subscription** at 5,000 to 12,000 rupees per year (ARPU benchmark range) for
   contact unlocking, AI compatibility reports, deeper verification tiers (employment,
   education, marital status), and the AI image-forensics report on one's own photos.
3. **Assisted tier** at 15,000 to 50,000 rupees with a human matchmaker augmented by
   Claude, with a partial success fee, aimed at the diaspora where willingness to pay and
   retention are highest.
